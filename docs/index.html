Kotoba

Kotoba is my personal project: an offline Android app built around the MDict dictionaries I already own. It keeps their original layout and media, reads text from games and comics, and turns the words I look up into flashcards. I'm working toward a version anyone can install; for now you can build it yourself and import your own dictionaries.

FlashcardsEvery word you look up can become a card

Save a word from a dictionary entry, a scanned game line or a comic bubble. The card keeps the dictionary's own formatting, so it looks like the page you learned it from.

  • Keep only the meanings you need, like just ② of 言葉, or write your own back.
  • Attach pronunciation. NHK clips show pitch accent: コトバ＼.
  • The sentence you found it in comes along as context.
  • FSRS-5 scheduling with learning steps, undo, a 7-day forecast and a streak.
  • Folders are decks. A whole dictionary list or one category becomes a deck in one tap.
  • Export to Anki (TSV with formatting) or CSV.

ScannerLook up text from another screen

Take a photo of a visual novel or game, or share a screenshot with Kotoba. The image stays on the phone while Kotoba finds the text.

  • Tap a text box and then a word to look it up, including conjugated forms.
  • Select area is handy when you only want the dialogue box.
  • It works with Japanese, simplified and traditional Chinese, Korean, Thai and Russian.
  • You can save the whole line with a card for context.

DictionariesKeep the layout of the original dictionary

Kotoba opens MDict .mdx and .mdd files, including Monokakido exports. It preserves each dictionary's styles, images and audio, and supports vertical layouts too.

  • Search headwords, definitions or examples.
  • Find dictionary forms such as 食べさせられなかった → 食べる and 추웠어요 → 춥다.
  • Open the dictionary's appendix (付録) with its original Japanese titles.
  • Or browse from one entry to the next, using an index rail to jump around.

Word listsBrowse the lists built into a dictionary

Kotoba exposes lists that are easy to miss in the original files: 大辞林's 地名, 人名 and 季語, 新明解's 最重要語 and 重要語, 漢検's 四字熟語 and levels, and the Korean dictionary's 品詞 and 専門用語, among others.

  • Choose a list or grid, and keep the original order or sort by reading.
  • Korean words can show hanja underneath; Japanese words can show readings.
  • Any list or category can become a flashcard deck.

Also includedReaders, reviews and kanji tools

Books and comicsRead EPUB, TXT and CBZ files, including Mihon downloads. In comics, tap recognized text inside a speech bubble to look it up.
Spaced repetitionReviews use FSRS-5. Folders work as decks, and cards can be exported to Anki or CSV.
Stays offlineThere is no account or internet permission. Even text recognition runs on the phone.

BuildBuild it yourself

To build Kotoba, you need Android SDK build-tools 35, platform 35 and JDK 21. The build does not use Gradle or require a network connection.

python3 android/build.py                 # → android/build/kotoba.apk
adb install -r android/build/kotoba.apk
android/tests/run.sh                     # desktop tests

Dictionaries are not included. On first run, go to Library → Import from a folder… and choose a folder containing your own MDict files.
