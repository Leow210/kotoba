Kotoba

An offline dictionary and flashcard app for Android, built on the MDict dictionaries you already own. Look words up in their original typography, scan the text in games and comics, and turn everything you find into spaced-repetition cards.

ScannerRead the text on any screen

Visual novels on a PC, games on a tablet, a screenshot someone sent you. Take a photo with the phone's camera, or share an image to Kotoba, and the text is read on the phone.

  • Tap a text box, then any word, to look it up. Conjugations are explained.
  • Select area reads just the dialogue box, in about 0.2 seconds.
  • Japanese, Chinese (simplified and traditional), Korean, Thai and Russian.
  • Save a card with the line as its example sentence.

DictionariesYour dictionaries, as they were printed

Kotoba reads MDict .mdx and .mdd files, such as Monokakido exports, and shows each dictionary with its own styles, images and audio, vertical ones included.

  • Headword, contains, in-definitions and in-examples search.
  • Dictionary-form lookup: 食べさせられなかった → 食べる, 추웠어요 → 춥다.
  • Each dictionary's appendix (付録) with its original Japanese titles.
  • Browse a whole dictionary like a paper one, with an index rail.

Word listsEvery list your dictionaries come with

大辞林's 地名, 人名 and 季語, 新明解's 最重要語 and 重要語, 漢検's 四字熟語 and levels, the Korean dictionary's 最重要語, 品詞 and 専門用語, and more. Each opens as a grid of categories.

  • List or grid, in the dictionary's order or あいう order.
  • Hanja under Korean words, readings under Japanese ones.
  • Turn any list or category into a flashcard deck in one tap.

And moreReading, reviewing, kanji

Books and comicsEPUB and TXT, vertical or horizontal. CBZ and Mihon downloads, with tap-to-look-up text in speech bubbles.
Spaced repetitionFSRS-5 scheduling, folders as decks, Anki (TSV) and CSV export, JSON backup.
Completely offlineNo account and no internet permission. Text recognition runs on the phone.

BuildBuild it yourself

Needs the Android SDK (build-tools 35, platform 35) and JDK 21. No Gradle, no network access during the build.

python3 android/build.py                 # → android/build/kotoba.apk
adb install -r android/build/kotoba.apk
android/tests/run.sh                     # desktop tests

Kotoba includes no dictionaries: it works with MDict files you own. On first run, go to Library → Import from a folder…
